## Props

<AutoTypeTable path="content/docs/props.ts" name="MuiRadioElementProps" cwd />

`MuiRadioElement` supports props of the MUI Radio component, along with additional adapter-specific props. For a complete list of available Radio properties, refer to the MUI [API documentation](https://mui.com/material-ui/api/radio/).

> The `ref` is forwarded to the underlying `<Radio />` component.

## Notes

- For custom radio UIs, build on `useMuiRadioAdapter`.
