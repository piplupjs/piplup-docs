`MuiTextFieldElement` supports props of the MUI TextField component, along with additional adapter-specific props. For a complete list of available TextField properties, refer to the MUI [API documentation](https://mui.com/material-ui/api/text-field/).

> The `ref` is forwarded to the underlying `<TextField />` component.

## Notes

- For custom text field UIs, build on `useMuiTextFieldAdapter`.
