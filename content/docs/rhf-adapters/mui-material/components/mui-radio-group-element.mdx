`MuiRadioGroupElement` supports props of the MUI RadioGroup component, along with additional adapter-specific props. For a complete list of available RadioGroup properties, refer to the MUI [API documentation](https://mui.com/material-ui/api/radio-group/).

> The `ref` is forwarded to the underlying `<RadioGroup />` component.

## Notes

- For custom radio-group UIs, build on `useMuiRadioGroupAdapter`.
