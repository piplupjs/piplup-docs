## Props

<AutoTypeTable path="content/docs/props.ts" name="MuiSliderElementProps" cwd />

`MuiSliderElement` supports props of the MUI Slider component, along with additional adapter-specific props. For a complete list of available Slider properties, refer to the MUI [API documentation](https://mui.com/material-ui/api/slider/).

> The `ref` is forwarded to the underlying `<Slider />` component.

## Notes

- For custom slider UIs, build on `useMuiSliderAdapter`.
