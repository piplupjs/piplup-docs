`MuiFormLabelElement` supports props of the MUI FormLabel component, along with additional adapter-specific props. For a complete list of available FormLabel properties, refer to the MUI [API documentation](https://mui.com/material-ui/api/form-label/).

> The `ref` is forwarded to the underlying `<FormLabel />` component.

## Notes

- For custom label UIs, build on `useMuiFormLabelAdapter`.
