## Props

<AutoTypeTable path="content/docs/props.ts" name="MuiRatingElementProps" cwd />

`MuiRatingElement` supports props of the MUI Rating component, along with additional adapter-specific props. For a complete list of available Rating properties, refer to the MUI [API documentation](https://mui.com/material-ui/api/rating/).

> The `ref` is forwarded to the underlying `<Rating />` component.

## Notes

- For custom rating UIs, build on `useMuiRatingAdapter`.
