`MuiFormHelperTextElement` supports all standard props of the MUI FormHelperText component, along with additional adapter-specific props. For a complete list of available FormHelperText properties, refer to the MUI [API documentation](https://mui.com/material-ui/api/form-helper-text/).

> The `ref` is forwarded to the underlying `<FormHelperText />` component.

## Notes

- For custom helper-text UIs, build on `useMuiFormHelperTextAdapter`.
