## Props

<AutoTypeTable path="content/docs/props.ts" name="MuiSwitchElementProps" cwd />

`MuiSwitchElement` supports props of the MUI Switch component, along with additional adapter-specific props. For a complete list of available Switch properties, refer to the MUI [API documentation](https://mui.com/material-ui/api/switch/).

> The `ref` is forwarded to the underlying `<Switch />` component.

## Notes

- For custom switch UIs, build on `useMuiSwitchAdapter`.
