## Props

<AutoTypeTable path="content/docs/props.ts" name="MuiFabElementProps" cwd />

`MuiFabElement` supports all standard props of the MUI Fab component, along with additional adapter-specific props. For a complete list of available Fab properties, refer to the MUI [API documentation](https://mui.com/material-ui/api/fab/).

> The `ref` is forwarded to the underlying `<Fab />` component.


## Notes

- For custom FAB UIs, build on `useMuiFabAdapter`.
